# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in a Heuro Studios project.

Most of our work is private, but for our public repos:

- **Issues** — Bug reports and ideas are welcome. Use the templates so we have
  what we need to act on them.
- **Pull requests** — For anything non-trivial, open an issue first so we can
  agree on the approach before you spend time on it. Keep PRs focused, and
  describe what changed and why.

We're a small team, so responses may take a little while — thanks for your patience.
