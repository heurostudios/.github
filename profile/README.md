# Heuro Studios

We're a Hull-based games and immersive studio. We build original games and
interactive experiences, and we use immersive technology to bring heritage and
culture to life.

### What we're working on

- 🎮 **Sinstruck** — our co-op horror game.
- 🏛️ **Immersive & heritage tech** — installations and experiences that
  reimagine how people engage with places, collections, and stories.
